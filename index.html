06_QUERY_RESOLUTION

Who can participate in CRYPTS'26? ▾
CRYPTS'26 is open to all students of OPG World School from Classes 4 through 12. Different events have specific class compatibility ranges — check each event card for eligibility details.
Can I register for multiple events? ▾
Yes — you can select multiple events during registration using the tag chip selector. However, ensure that selected events do not have conflicting time slots in the schedule before enrolling.
Is registration free? ▾
Registration for CRYPTS'26 is completely free for all OPG World School students. Simply fill out the enrollment form on this site and await your confirmation email.
How will I know if my registration was successful? ▾
Upon successful submission, you will receive a confirmation email at the address provided during registration. You can also check the terminal output on this site immediately after submitting for confirmation.
Are the events individual or team-based? ▾
Most events in CRYPTS'26 are individually registered — however, certain events like CIRCUIT_BREAKER may allow team participation. Refer to the Event Rulebook in the Resource Matrix for specific team size allowances per event.
What should I bring on the day of the fest? ▾
Bring your school ID card and a printed or digital copy of your registration confirmation. Additional materials vary by event — consult the Participant Guide in the Resource Matrix section for event-specific requirements.
Will there be prizes? ▾
No Cash prizes or trophies as of now. Only certificates, no special award either
Who do I contact for queries? ▾
Reach out to the organizing team directly through your class teacher or the CRYPTS organizing committee. For fast email resolution, email our faculty coordinator at ankitayadav@opgworldschool.com or contact the student event in-charges. You can also type help or about in the CLI terminal.
✉️ DIRECT EMAIL SUPPORT FOR QUERIES: Having trouble navigating the terminal or need instant query resolution? Contact our Faculty Coordinator directly.
